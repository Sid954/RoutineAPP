# v1.5.23

## New
- **Swipe between days in notification settings.** Tap the bell beside the day title and swipe sideways: the page follows your finger and slides to the next day. The daily routine behind it moves to the same day.
- **Everything animates.** Switches, chips, weekday buttons and sound choices now fade between states instead of snapping.

## Fixed
- **Corner icons on the week strip** (cancelled, test, online and so on) no longer get cut off for half a second when you move between dates.
- **A wrong "Dock covered by..." warning** no longer appears when you tap Share or other buttons on a notice.
