# v1.5.20

## New
- **Settings in groups.** Settings is now a list of groups (Appearance, Home, Class cards, Routine, Time, Gestures and more). Open a group to change it, search for any setting, and reset everything in one tap. You can change the accent colour, corner roundness, text size, 12 or 24 hour time, and what each class card shows.
- **Cleaner Home.** The date bar is one card with the chosen day highlighted, and it shows the month on the 1st. Classes that follow each other join into one block, with the breaks between blocks.
- **Tap the little icons on the date bar** to see what is happening that day. Tap a row to open that notice.
- **Campus closed.** A day with no campus now says "Campus closed" in big letters and every class is greyed out like a cancelled one. The date is crossed out on the bar, and days with no classes say "No classes".
- **"Back to today" only when you need it.** It shows when you look at a different week (Thursday to Wednesday), and you can turn it off in Settings.
- **Routine page.** Day tabs show the date and what is on, with a short summary for each day. You can edit a class right where it is.
- **Apps and Notices** have a new, calmer look. New notices stay marked "new" until you leave the Notices page, "Mark all read" is one tap, pinned notices stay open, and past notices only show a count once you open Past.
- **Smoother Notices.** Groups open and close, and category chips grow and shrink, with a short animation.
- **Posting a notice.** "Notice" is now first in the list of what you can post, and the other names are shorter and clearer.

## Fixed
- The "Copied to clipboard" message now shows above the notice sheet instead of behind it.
