# v1.5.24

## New
- **Confirmed and not-confirmed icons.** The words "Confirmed by votes", "Unconfirmed" and "Not confirmed" are now icons in the top right corner: a green check for a notice classmates confirmed, an amber cross for a suggestion still being voted on. Tap one to see what it means and why, and to jump to that notice or suggestion.
- **Cancelled classes look calmer.** On Home, a cancelled class is now a greyed-out box with a struck-through name, the same as "Campus closed".

## Changed
- **Weekly Routine is plain.** It shows your routine only: no cancellations, holidays, exams or notices appear there. Home still shows them.
