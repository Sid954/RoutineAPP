# My Routine v1.5.12 Release Notes

New notifications, made to fit your day.

### What you get
- **Class reminders:** "DSL in 10 min" with the room, teacher and time. Pick 5 to 30 minutes, a different time for labs, every class or just the first one, and an optional alert when a class starts. Cancelled classes, holidays and moved classes are skipped on their own.
- **Tests:** an earlier reminder before a test class, plus a heads-up the evening before.
- **Morning summary:** today's classes, cancellations, tests and deadlines at a time you choose.
- **Tomorrow preview:** the evening before, so you know what's coming.
- **Assignment deadlines:** a reminder hours before something is due. Tap Done to stop it.
- **Notices:** choose which kinds alert you (cancelled, holiday, tests, online, moved, assignments, general).

### Quick control
- **Pause:** 1 hour, 3 hours, the rest of today, or until you turn it back on.
- **Skip a class:** right from the notification, or with the new Reminder switch in a class's details.
- **Coming up:** see today's and tomorrow's alerts and switch any of them, or a whole day, off.
- **Days and quiet hours:** pick the days you want reminders, and quiet hours when nothing makes a sound.

Find it all in **Settings → Notifications**. Reminders now arrive even when the app is closed, and after your phone restarts.

### Testing (temporary)
- **Test time:** tap the small "Test time" tab at the top of any page, then pick a day and drag the time slider. The app follows live. Tap "Real time" to go back. Notifications are paused while it's on.
