# v1.5.25

## Rooms
- **Calendar button.** A calendar in the top right lets you pick any date, not just this week.
- **Clock button.** A clock in the top right lets you type any time, or tap a class slot, to see which rooms are free then. Tapping the "Now" time does the same. Both buttons light up when you are not looking at the live day or time.
- **Rooms in number order.** Inside each floor the rooms now go 403, 404, 405 and so on, and no longer jump around as rooms free up.

## Fixed
- **No wrong "Now" on other dates.** Looking at another day in a room no longer marks the 8:30 class as "Now".
- **Cancelled classes can be tapped** in a room's schedule, and open their details.
- The "Dock covered by..." message no longer pops up.
