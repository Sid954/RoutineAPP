# v1.5.18

## New Notices page
- **Your section only.** Notices for your section, plus anything sent to everyone.
- **Two tabs:** Notices and Suggestions, each with a count.
- **Clearer cards.** Each says what happened once: "ICMP class cancelled", "DMNT class test (CT-2)", "CPL assignment: Lab report 5". The background colour shows the type, the day is top right, and who posted it is bottom right.
- **Join and Upload buttons.** Online classes with a link get a Join button right on the card. Assignments can now include an upload link and get an Upload button.
- **Small icon filters.** Tap one to see only that type. Its name shows while it is selected.
- **Folding sections.** Today, Tomorrow and anything new are open. The rest fold away.
- **A number on the Notices button** in the bottom bar shows what is new. Opening Notices takes you to the first new one.
- **Hide option** for the "notifications are blocked" message, and a settings button next to the Notices title.

## Suggestions
- Vote with up and down arrows. The score shows in between.
- Each suggestion shows "Unconfirmed" and how many of the 8 votes it has.

## Posting
- Tap **New** and follow the steps: suggest it or announce it, what it is about, the details, then check it before sending.
- Your name fills in by itself. Only Announce asks for the posting password.
