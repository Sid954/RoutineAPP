# My Routine v1.5.13 Release Notes

The website now works properly on a computer.

### Mouse and trackpad
- **Change days:** drag the schedule left or right, swipe sideways with two fingers on a trackpad, or press the ← and → keys.
- **Close sheets:** drag class, room, notice and teacher details down to close them, just like on the phone.
- **Refresh:** drag down from the top of Home, Notices, Faculty or Rooms.
- **Sideways rows:** filters, "Coming up" and the Weekly Routine day tabs scroll with the mouse wheel, or by dragging.
- **No accidental taps:** dragging over a class no longer opens it, and dragging never selects text.

Nothing changes on your phone; touch works exactly as before.

### Testing (temporary)
- **Test time:** tap the small "Test time" tab at the top of any page, then pick a day and drag the time slider. The app follows live. Tap "Real time" to go back. Notifications are paused while it's on.
