# v1.5.19

## Fixed
- **Smoother Notices.** Every 30 seconds the app re-drew the whole page even when nothing had changed, which caused a small freeze. It now only redraws when something is new.
- **Hidden messages no longer sit over the bottom bar.** The pop-up messages at the bottom are now fully out of the way when they are not showing.

## Help us find the stuck bottom bar
- If you tap the bottom bar and something else is on top of it, a message now says what it is ("Dock covered by …"). Send a screenshot of that message.
